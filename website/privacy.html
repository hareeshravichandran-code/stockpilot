1. Who we are

Kanalyst is a personal and family finance app built and run in India. It helps you track investments, goals, retirement, loans, insurance, documents and important dates for the people in your household. Kanalyst is an information tool. It is not a bank, broker or registered investment adviser, and nothing in it is financial advice.

3. How we use it

  • To show your net worth, holdings, spending, goals, retirement plan, family cover and calendar.
  • To remind you about renewals, premiums, fees and maturities.
  • To keep the web app and Android app in sync for your account.
  • To fix errors and keep the service secure.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models.

4. Bank SMS (Android)

With your permission, the Android app reads SMS from known bank and card sender IDs to record transactions. It does not read personal messages, OTPs or messages from your contacts. Transaction details (amount, merchant, date, bank) are stored on your phone and synced to your Kanalyst account. You can turn off SMS access at any time in your phone’s settings. Transactions you added by hand are not affected.

5. Gmail integration: Limited Use disclosure

The web app can optionally connect to your Gmail account, with the read-only gmail.readonly permission, to import your investment statements automatically.

  • What we look for: statements from the depositories (NSDL, CDSL, CVL), NPS statements from Protean, and income or dividend emails matching rules you set up.
  • What we keep: only the figures extracted from those statements, such as holdings, values and dates. We don’t keep the emails themselves.
  • Password-protected PDFs: to open a statement, we may use the PAN, date of birth or mobile number you saved in Profile. When a password can’t be worked out by simple rules, the email’s sender, subject and body text and those profile details may be sent to Google’s Gemini API to suggest the password. Some PDFs may also be read using Google’s text-recognition services. This processing happens under Google’s API terms.
  • What we never do: send, delete, label or change your email; read unrelated personal email; or let a human read your email, except with your explicit consent for a specific support request, or where the law requires it.
  • Token storage: your Gmail access tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are stored.
  • Turning it off: use Settings → Privacy → Disconnect Gmail, or remove Kanalyst at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting revokes our access at Google and deletes the stored tokens.

Kanalyst’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Google Sheets

If you connect Google Sheets, we ask permission to write to the spreadsheet you choose, to export your data. We don’t read other files in your Google Drive. You can revoke this at any time from your Google Account.

7. Family sharing

You can invite your partner or other adults into a family group. People in the same family group can see each other’s shared information, such as combined holdings, goals, insurance, documents and calendar dates. Only the person who added an item can change or delete it. You can leave a family group, or remove someone from it, at any time.

When you add family members who don’t have their own account, such as children or parents, you are responsible for having their permission, or the authority to share their details on their behalf.

8. Storage and security

Your data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database, and the service runs on Railway, Vercel and Netlify. All traffic uses TLS (HTTPS). Data is encrypted at rest by our database provider, and Gmail tokens get an extra layer of AES-256-GCM encryption. Access to your data requires your signed-in session. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you promptly if a breach affects you.

9. Who we share with

We don’t sell or rent your data. We share it only:

  • with the service providers that run Kanalyst (Supabase, Railway, Vercel, Netlify, Google), only to provide the service;
  • with people in your family group, as described above;
  • when the law requires it.

10. Your rights

You can see, correct, export or delete your data. You can withdraw any permission (SMS, Gmail, Sheets) at any time. To ask for a copy of your data, write to us at the address below. We follow India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

11. Deleting your data

In the web app, go to Settings → Privacy → Delete Account & All Data and type DELETE to confirm. This revokes Gmail access and permanently removes your account and everything linked to it: holdings, transactions, goals, Blueprint boards, retirement plan, family members, policies, documents and calendar entries. On Android, use Settings → Privacy & Permissions → Delete All My Data. Deletion can’t be undone.

12. Children

Kanalyst accounts are for adults (18 or older). Parents may record details about their children, such as name, birthday, school fees, savings and insurance, inside their own account. Children do not get their own accounts.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the changes are significant, we will tell you in the app before they apply.

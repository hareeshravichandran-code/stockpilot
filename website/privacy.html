1. Who we are

Kanalyst is an independent product, built and run from Chennai, India, by Hareesh Ravichandran (the "data fiduciary" under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023). It shows a family what it owns, what it owes and what is due, from bank SMS and statements.

Kanalyst is an information tool. It is not a bank, broker, payment app or registered investment adviser, and nothing in it is financial advice. Contact: hello@kanalyst.in.

3. End-to-end encryption

The fields marked "yes" above are encrypted on your phone or in your browser (AES-256-GCM) before they reach our servers. Our database only holds unreadable text such as ev1:Xq9…. Kanalyst staff and our hosting providers cannot read them.

  • Where your key lives: in a hidden app folder of your own Google Drive (only Kanalyst can see it there) and, on Android, protected by your phone's screen lock. You can also set a passphrase or keep a recovery code.
  • New phone or browser: sign in to the same Google account and your data opens as before.
  • What stays readable to the server, and why: amounts, dates, categories and fingerprints of payee words, so totals, reminders and "this bill is paid" work without seeing names.
  • If you lose every key (Google account, phone, passphrase and recovery code), encrypted fields cannot be recovered, by you or by us.

4. Bank SMS (Android)

Reading bank SMS is the app's core feature: it is how payments, bills and balances are recorded without typing. With your permission (READ_SMS and RECEIVE_SMS):

  • The app looks through SMS on your phone to find bank, card, UPI, loan and investment transaction messages. OTPs, promotions and personal messages are recognised and skipped on the phone; they are never uploaded.
  • From each transaction SMS it keeps the amount, date, bank, payee and the message text. The payee and text are encrypted on the phone before syncing.
  • It never sends SMS, never reads contacts, and never uses SMS for advertising.
  • You can turn SMS access off any time in Android Settings → Apps → Kanalyst → Permissions. Data already recorded stays until you delete it.

5. How we use it

  • To show your family's spending, balances, investments, net worth and what is due.
  • To remind you before bills, EMIs, premiums and renewals, and to stop the reminder when the payment SMS arrives.
  • To keep your phone and the web app in sync.
  • To fix errors and keep the service secure.

We do not use your data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not use it to train AI models.

6. Gmail integration: Limited Use disclosure

The web app can optionally connect to Gmail with the read-only gmail.readonly permission, to import investment statements.

  • What we look for: statements from NSDL, CDSL and CAMS/KFintech (CAS), NPS statements, and income or dividend emails matching rules you set.
  • What we keep: only figures from those statements (holdings, values, dates). Not the emails.
  • Password-protected PDFs: opened with the PAN, date of birth or mobile in your Profile. When simple rules can't work out the password, the sender, subject and body of that statement email may be sent to Google's Gemini API to suggest one, under Google's API terms.
  • Never: send, delete or change email; read unrelated email; or let a person read your email, except with your consent for a support request or where the law requires.
  • Tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Disconnect any time in Settings → Privacy, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions; this revokes access and deletes the tokens.

Kanalyst's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Google Sheets and Drive

If you export to Google Sheets, we write only to the spreadsheet you choose. For encryption, Kanalyst uses the hidden app-data folder of your Drive (drive.appdata) to keep your key. It cannot see any of your other Drive files, and the Kanalyst server never gets Drive access.

8. Family sharing

You can invite your partner or other adults into a family group. People in the group see what is shared: combined balances, holdings, insurance, documents and dates. Only the person who added an item can change or delete it. You can leave or remove someone any time. When you add family members without their own account (children, parents), you must have their permission or the authority to add their details.

9. Crash reports (Android)

If the app crashes or a screen hits an error, it sends us: the error type, the error message with every number removed, the lines of our own code involved, the app version, phone model, Android version and the screen name. Never SMS text, amounts, payee names, account numbers or contacts. This is so we can fix problems without asking you for a report.

10. Where it is kept

Data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database; the service runs on Railway, Vercel and Netlify. All traffic uses HTTPS (TLS). Data is encrypted at rest by the database provider, and the fields in section 2 are additionally end-to-end encrypted. Access needs your signed-in session. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

11. Who we share with

We don't sell or rent your data. It is shared only:

  • with the providers that run Kanalyst (Supabase, Railway, Vercel, Netlify, Google), only to provide the service;
  • with people in your family group, as in section 8;
  • when the law requires it.

12. How long we keep it

Your data is kept while your account exists and deleted when you delete the account. Crash reports are deleted after 90 days. Server logs (errors and request times, without your personal content) are kept for a short time by our hosting provider and then deleted automatically.

13. Your rights

You can see, correct, export and delete your data, and withdraw any permission (SMS, Gmail, Sheets, Drive) at any time. For a copy of your data or a grievance, write to hello@kanalyst.in; we reply within 30 days.

14. Deleting your data

  • Android: Settings → Delete Account & All Data.
  • Web: app.kanalyst.in → Settings → Privacy → Delete account, type DELETE.
  • Without the app: follow kanalyst.in/delete-account.

Deletion revokes Gmail access and removes your account and everything linked to it straight away: transactions, rules, what's due, investments, family members, policies, documents and crash reports linked to your account. It can't be undone.

15. Children

Accounts are for adults (18+). Parents may record their children's details (birthday, fees, savings, insurance) inside their own account. Children do not get accounts.

16. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes. For significant changes we tell you in the app before they apply.

17. Contact

Questions, data requests or grievances: hello@kanalyst.in · Kanalyst, Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India.

Delete your Kanalyst account

Kanalyst (Android app com.kanalyst.app and app.kanalyst.in), by Hareesh Ravichandran, Chennai, India. You can delete your account and all its data at any time. It is free and can't be undone.

In the Android app

  1. Open Kanalyst and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account & All Data.
  3. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted straight away and you are signed out.

On the web

  1. Sign in at app.kanalyst.in.
  2. Go to Settings → Privacy → Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm.

Without the app

Email hello@kanalyst.in from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Kanalyst account". We confirm it is you, delete the account within 7 days, and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

Everything linked to your account: profile, transactions and SMS text, SMS rules and groups, what's due (bills, EMIs, subscriptions), investments and statements, family members, insurance policies, documents, dates, support messages and crash reports. Gmail access is revoked and its tokens deleted.

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you. Server logs without personal content expire automatically after a short time. Your encryption key in your own Google Drive app folder is yours: remove it from Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Kanalyst → Delete hidden app data.
